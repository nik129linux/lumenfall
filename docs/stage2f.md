# Stage 2f — Clean minion frames

The minion sprites (`sprites/wisp.png`, `crawler.png`, `spitter.png` + `sprites/manifest.json`)
are generated by `tools/cut_minions.py` from Nico's sheets in `assets/` (read-only). 11 of
26 frames contain slivers of the NEIGHBOURING frame or a vertical cut line, so in game a
creature drags a stray piece of another pose. Fix the cutter, regenerate the sprites.

Done when `python3 tools/gate2f.py` prints GATE PASS (it lists every bad frame).

## Files you may edit
`tools/cut_minions.py`, and the generated files it writes (`sprites/*.png`, `sprites/manifest.json`). Nothing else.

## Method (do this, do not invent another)
1. For each frame, the box is the cell between the gaps of the sheet; do NOT crop by fixed
   pixel boxes that can overlap the neighbour.
2. Build the alpha mask for the whole SHEET first (magenta key for wisp/spitter; for the
   crawler sheet: near-black low-saturation background, flood-filled from the borders).
3. Label connected components (8-connectivity) on the whole-sheet mask. Assign every
   component to the frame whose column/row range contains most of its area.
4. A frame = the union of the components assigned to it. Components smaller than 6 pixels
   that touch no other piece of that frame are dropped. Nothing from another frame may enter.
5. A wide creature (crawler lunge, wisp dash trail) is still ONE component or a few pieces
   that all sit inside that frame's own range: that is fine, the gate only complains about
   pieces that belong to the neighbour.
6. Keep the cell size, display size, clip names and frame order exactly as they are now.
7. After regenerating, run `node tools/stage2e.mjs` if it still works (it must not crash)
   and look at a contact sheet of all frames on grey before finishing.
